# Idea Origin

## Problem Observation
Students commonly receive a fixed amount of pocket money but make many small purchases during the month. Because individual purchases feel small, they are easy to forget.

## User Pain Point
At the end of the month, the student may know that the money is gone but not know:
- What they spent it on
- Which category cost the most
- How much they spend each week
- How much money they had left before the next pocket-money cycle

## Opportunity
A lightweight tracker can turn individual transactions into a simple visual summary. The focus is not complex personal finance management; it is awareness and better everyday money habits.

## Target User
Primary:
- School and college students
- Students who receive pocket money
- Students who want a simple spending overview

## Why This Idea
Existing finance applications can feel too complex for someone who only wants to track small daily expenses. This project focuses on speed, simplicity, and immediate visual feedback.

## Product Principle
“Record it quickly. Understand it instantly.”

## Assumptions
- Users have a smartphone or browser.
- Users are willing to enter expenses manually.
- Most users need only basic categories.
- Privacy is important, so the MVP can keep data locally on the device.
